# ADR 0009 — Deterministic local life and audio timing

Status: accepted.

Local life uses an injected monotonic clock and seeded randomness. Frame-rate-independent interpolation produces calm blinking, breathing, saccades and posture. Outgoing audio energy drives V0 mouth movement through a pluggable lip-sync contract.

Consequence: no AI is needed for perceived life. Seeds reproduce defects. Audio remains the clock and never waits for animation. Future visemes plug into the driver boundary without changing provider or controller semantics.
