# Product origin

AMYU is pronounced “AM-you” and carries the internal concept “A Mind, Yours.” It is the runtime and product identity, distinct from the companion's user-chosen personal name.

The long-term feeling is an embodied, persistent personal companion that knows the user, remembers ongoing work and helps with explicitly permitted computer context and actions. The first success is local life before a model answers anything. This repository starts with that foundation and stops before realtime AI.

The development artwork and all future assets require recorded provenance. The production character and sonic identity should be original. Do not copy JARVIS/film dialogue, music, sound design or artwork.
